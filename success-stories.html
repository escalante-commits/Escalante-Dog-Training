Callie's Story

Callie

Every training journey starts somewhere. Callie's story is a reminder that with patience, consistency, and clear communication, dogs can work toward a brighter future.

"We are so grateful to Nicole for helping us give Callie the life she deserves! Callie was being fostered and was at risk of being abandoned when we saw Nicole's video of her. We immediately knew we wanted to give her a loving forever home, and we adopted her. Callie is dog-aggressive, which has presented some challenges, but Nicole has continued to support us every step of the way. She has taught us how to safely and successfully walk Callie, better understand her needs, and build the confidence to help her thrive. Thanks to Nicole's patience, knowledge, and continued guidance, we have learned so much about how to work with Callie and set her up for success. We are so thankful for everything Nicole has done for Callie and our family. She truly cares about the dogs she works with, and we couldn't recommend her enough!" -Julie (Callie's Mom)
